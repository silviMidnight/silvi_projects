# Privacy Policy for GoCurr

**Last updated: April 10, 2026**

## Overview

GoCurr ("the App") is a currency converter application. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App.

## Data Collection

**GoCurr does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.** The App does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information.

## What the App Does

- The App fetches publicly available currency exchange rates from third-party APIs to provide conversion functionality.
- Your favorite currency pairs and preferences are stored **locally on your device only** and are never transmitted to any server.

## Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party APIs solely to retrieve current exchange rate data. No personal information is sent to these services.

## Analytics and Tracking

GoCurr does **not** use any analytics, tracking, or advertising frameworks. We do not track your usage, location, or any other personal data.

## Data Storage

All user preferences (such as favorite currency pairs) are stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
